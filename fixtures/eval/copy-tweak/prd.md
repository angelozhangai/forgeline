# Requirement: Home page banner copy tweak

## Background
Ops reports the current main title on the home page's top banner, "Start your creative journey", is too generic and click-through is low.

## Scope
- Change the top banner's main title from "Start your creative journey" to "Make your first short film in 3 minutes".
- Copy change only — the banner's image, target link, styling, and position all stay the same.
- Update both locales (the Chinese locale changes to the equivalent translated line).

## Acceptance
- The home page banner shows the new main title; the old copy no longer appears.
- The click-through target is identical to before the change.
